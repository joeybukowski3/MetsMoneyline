Privacy Policy

This page explains what limited data MetsMoneyline may collect, why it is collected, and how readers can contact the site with privacy questions.

Analytics and Technical Data

MetsMoneyline uses standard web analytics and hosting logs to understand site traffic, page usage, and technical performance. This may include browser type, device type, pages visited, timestamps, and referral information.

Email Subscriptions

If you subscribe to MetsMoneyline emails, your email address is handled through the site’s third-party email provider. MetsMoneyline uses that information only to deliver requested site updates and related communications.

Third-Party Services

The site may use third-party tools for analytics, email delivery, affiliate tracking, and hosting. Those providers may process technical information according to their own policies.

Your Choices

If you have questions about your information or want to stop receiving emails, contact the site through the Support page or use the unsubscribe option included in email communications.
