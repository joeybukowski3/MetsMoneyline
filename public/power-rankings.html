How the Model Works

Each category is scored 0–100 via percentile rank across all 30 teams (higher is always better — ERA/WHIP/errors are inverted). Weighted scores are summed to produce a final 0–100 power score. Recent Form is based on each team's last 20 games W/L. Score Δ shows the change from the previous run. Strength of Schedule is approximated by division (AL/NL East toughest; Central softest) — a known limitation noted in the methodology. Injury adjustment is reserved for a future enhancement and currently contributes a neutral 50 pts.
