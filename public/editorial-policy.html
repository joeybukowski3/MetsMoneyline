Editorial Policy

MetsMoneyline aims to be transparent about what is data-driven, what is judgment, and what the limits of any one-game betting analysis are.

Source Standards

Reports are built from site-generated and cached baseball data such as lineups, pitching indicators, bullpen context, odds, and team trend files already used across MetsMoneyline. The site avoids unsupported claims and does not present guesses as facts.

Pick Framing

MetsMoneyline is a Mets-focused site, so the official pick is framed around the Mets side. That does not mean every game is treated as equally strong. When data is mixed, the site is expected to say so plainly and explain where the risk sits.

Corrections

If factual errors are discovered in public content or generated summaries, the site should correct them in source or published output as quickly as practical.

Responsible Language

MetsMoneyline avoids promising wins or using language that implies certainty. Sports outcomes are uncertain, and analysis should remain grounded in the available context.
